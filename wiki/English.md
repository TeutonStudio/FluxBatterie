# Flux Battery Wiki

[Deutsch](Home) · **English**

Flux Battery connects a portable battery to **Flux Networks** and **Mekanism**. In a discharging slot it powers machines as a virtual **Flux Point**; in a charging slot it sends surplus energy into the network as a virtual **Flux Plug**.

Published baseline: **0.1.3-beta.2** · Minecraft **1.21.1** · NeoForge **21.1.231+ within 21.1** · Java **21**.
